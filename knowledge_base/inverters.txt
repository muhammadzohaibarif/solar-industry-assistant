SOLAR INVERTERS

Definition:

A solar inverter is an electrical device that converts direct current (DC) electricity generated by solar panels or stored in batteries into alternating current (AC) electricity used by many electrical appliances and systems.

Main Functions:

- Convert DC electricity to AC electricity
- Manage solar power generation
- Monitor system performance
- Provide protection functions
- Coordinate with batteries and the grid when supported

Common Types:

1. On-grid inverter
2. Off-grid inverter
3. Hybrid inverter
4. Microinverter

On-Grid Inverter:

An on-grid inverter is designed to operate with a utility electricity grid.

Characteristics:

- Converts solar DC into AC
- Works with grid-connected solar systems
- Usually does not require batteries
- Can support export of excess electricity where permitted

Off-Grid Inverter:

An off-grid inverter is designed for systems that operate independently of the utility grid.

Characteristics:

- Works with battery storage
- Supplies AC electricity to loads
- Manages energy from solar panels and batteries
- Suitable for systems without reliable grid access

Hybrid Inverter:

A hybrid inverter can coordinate solar generation, battery storage, loads, and grid electricity depending on its design.

Potential Functions:

- Solar energy conversion
- Battery charging
- Battery discharging
- Grid interaction
- Backup power
- Energy management

Microinverters:

Microinverters are installed at or near individual solar panels and convert DC to AC at the panel level.

They can be useful in systems with shading or complex roof layouts, depending on the design.

Important Specifications:

- Rated power
- Maximum PV input power
- Maximum DC voltage
- MPPT voltage range
- Maximum input current
- AC output voltage
- Efficiency
- Number of MPPTs
- Battery compatibility
- Communication features
- Protection features
- Warranty

Inverter Sizing:

The inverter should be appropriately matched with the solar array and electrical loads.

Inverter selection depends on:

- Solar panel capacity
- Maximum load
- Daily energy consumption
- Battery requirements
- System type
- Grid requirements
- Future expansion
- Manufacturer specifications

Important:

A larger inverter is not automatically better. The inverter must be compatible with the panels, batteries, loads, and electrical system.

Customer Questions:

Customers may ask about inverter types, inverter capacity, hybrid inverters, battery compatibility, backup capability, efficiency, brands, and inverter sizing.

Important Limitation:

Final inverter selection should be confirmed by a qualified solar professional using the complete system design and equipment specifications.
