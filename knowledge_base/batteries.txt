SOLAR BATTERIES

Definition:

A solar battery stores electrical energy so that it can be used when solar generation is unavailable or when backup power is required.

Main Uses:

- Energy storage
- Backup power
- Night-time energy use
- Supporting off-grid and hybrid systems
- Reducing dependence on the grid where applicable

Common Battery Types:

1. Lead-acid batteries
2. Lithium-ion batteries

Lead-Acid:

Lead-acid batteries are a traditional energy-storage technology. They can have a lower initial cost but generally require more maintenance and have different depth-of-discharge characteristics than lithium batteries.

Lithium-Ion:

Lithium-ion batteries are widely used in modern energy-storage systems. They generally offer high energy density, longer cycle life, and lower maintenance requirements compared with traditional lead-acid batteries.

Important Specifications:

- Battery capacity
- Voltage
- Usable energy
- Depth of discharge
- Cycle life
- Charging requirements
- Compatibility with inverter

Battery Selection:

Selection depends on:

- Required backup time
- Electrical loads
- Energy consumption
- System type
- Budget
- Available space
- Inverter compatibility

Backup Calculation:

Required battery capacity depends on the power of the loads that need backup and the required backup duration.

Customer Questions:

Customers may ask about battery types, capacity, backup time, battery life, compatibility, installation, maintenance, and warranty.

Important Limitation:

Actual battery sizing and selection should be verified by a qualified solar professional using the complete electrical load and system design.
