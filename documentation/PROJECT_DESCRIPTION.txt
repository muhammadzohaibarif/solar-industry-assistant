SOLAR INDUSTRY ASSISTANT
PROJECT DESCRIPTION
===================

Solar Industry Assistant is an AI-powered solar energy application designed to provide solar-related information, calculations, recommendations, and customer assistance through a web-based interface.

The system combines a locally hosted Llama 3.2 Large Language Model through Ollama with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), a structured solar knowledge base, rule-based calculation tools, conversation memory, a Flask REST API, SQLite storage, and a responsive frontend.

The assistant can answer natural-language questions about solar panels, batteries, inverters, installation, maintenance, warranties, pricing concepts, energy consumption, and solar systems.

It also provides practical calculation capabilities including solar panel sizing, battery sizing, battery backup time, inverter sizing, and appliance energy consumption.

The recommendation engine can determine whether an ON-GRID, OFF-GRID, or HYBRID solar system may be appropriate based on grid availability, reliability, backup requirements, and battery requirements.

The application runs locally, reducing dependence on external AI APIs while providing an interactive solar assistant through a browser-based interface.
