SOLAR ENERGY CONSUMPTION

Definition:

Energy consumption is the amount of electrical energy used by appliances and electrical equipment over a period of time.

The common unit for electricity consumption is kilowatt-hour (kWh).

Basic Formula:

Energy Consumption (Wh) = Power (W) × Operating Time (hours)

For kilowatt-hours:

Energy Consumption (kWh) = Power (kW) × Operating Time (hours)

Example:

A 1000 W appliance operating for 2 hours consumes approximately:

1000 × 2 = 2000 Wh

or

2 kWh

Daily Energy Consumption:

Daily consumption can be estimated by calculating the energy used by each appliance and adding the results.

Example:

Fan:
100 W × 8 hours = 800 Wh

Refrigerator:
150 W × 10 hours equivalent = 1500 Wh

Lights:
20 W × 5 hours = 100 Wh

Total estimated consumption:

2400 Wh = 2.4 kWh per day

Important Loads:

Solar system planning may consider:

- Lights
- Fans
- Refrigerators
- TVs
- Computers
- Washing machines
- Water pumps
- Air conditioners
- Motors
- Kitchen appliances
- Other electrical equipment

Load Information:

For each appliance, collect:

- Appliance name
- Rated power
- Quantity
- Hours of operation per day
- Starting or surge requirements where applicable

Peak Load:

Peak load is the highest electrical demand occurring when multiple appliances operate simultaneously.

Peak load is important when selecting an inverter.

Energy Consumption and Solar Sizing:

Daily energy consumption is one of the important inputs used to estimate the required solar generation capacity.

Actual generation depends on:

- Solar irradiation
- Location
- Panel orientation
- Panel tilt
- Shading
- Temperature
- System losses
- Equipment efficiency

Customer Questions:

Customers may ask how to calculate electricity consumption, how many units they use, what appliances consume the most electricity, and how consumption affects solar sizing.

Important Limitation:

Energy consumption estimates should be based on actual electricity bills, appliance ratings, measured usage, or professional load assessment where possible.
