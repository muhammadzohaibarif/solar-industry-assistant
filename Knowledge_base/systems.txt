SOLAR SYSTEM TYPES

Solar systems can generally be classified into three main types:

1. ON-GRID SYSTEM
2. OFF-GRID SYSTEM
3. HYBRID SYSTEM

ON-GRID SYSTEM:

An on-grid solar system is connected to the utility electricity grid.

Characteristics:

- Solar panels generate electricity during suitable sunlight conditions.
- The system works with the utility grid.
- Battery storage is not necessarily required.
- Excess generation may be handled according to applicable grid arrangements and regulations.

Advantages:

- Suitable for locations with reliable grid access
- Can reduce electricity purchased from the grid
- Usually simpler than systems with battery storage

Limitations:

- Grid-connected systems generally depend on grid availability for normal operation.
- Backup capability depends on the specific system design.

OFF-GRID SYSTEM:

An off-grid solar system operates independently of the utility grid.

Characteristics:

- Solar panels provide electricity.
- Batteries store energy when solar generation is insufficient.
- An inverter manages conversion and supply.

Advantages:

- Useful where grid access is unavailable or unreliable
- Can provide energy independence when properly designed

Limitations:

- Requires appropriate battery storage
- Generation and storage must be correctly sized

HYBRID SYSTEM:

A hybrid solar system combines solar generation, battery storage, and grid interaction depending on the design.

Characteristics:

- Solar panels generate electricity.
- Batteries can store energy.
- Grid power can be used when required.
- Backup can be available for selected loads depending on configuration.

Advantages:

- Greater flexibility
- Backup capability
- Combines solar, battery, and grid electricity

Limitations:

- More components and control requirements
- Usually greater complexity and cost

SYSTEM SELECTION:

The appropriate system depends on:

- Grid availability
- Electricity consumption
- Backup requirements
- Battery requirements
- Budget
- Major electrical loads
- Available installation area

Customer Questions:

Customers may ask about on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid systems, suitability, backup capability, batteries, and system costs.

Important Limitation:

Final system selection should be confirmed by a qualified solar professional after evaluating the site, electrical loads, grid conditions, and applicable requirements.
