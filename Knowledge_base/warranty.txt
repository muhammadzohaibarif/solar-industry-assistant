SOLAR WARRANTY

Definition:

A solar warranty is a manufacturer's or installer's commitment covering specified products or services for a defined period under stated terms and conditions.

Common Warranty Categories:

1. Product warranty
2. Performance warranty
3. Inverter warranty
4. Battery warranty
5. Installation workmanship warranty

Panel Product Warranty:

Covers specified manufacturing or material defects according to manufacturer terms.

Panel Performance Warranty:

May specify minimum expected power output over a defined period.

Inverter Warranty:

Covers specified inverter defects and failures according to manufacturer terms.

Battery Warranty:

Battery warranties may specify:

- Warranty duration
- Cycle limitations
- Throughput limitations
- Operating conditions
- Depth-of-discharge conditions
- Installation requirements

Installation Warranty:

An installer may provide a separate workmanship warranty covering installation-related issues.

Important Warranty Conditions:

Warranty coverage may depend on:

- Proper installation
- Correct operating conditions
- Product registration
- Maintenance requirements
- Authorized service procedures
- Environmental conditions
- Manufacturer terms

Customer Questions:

Customers may ask about warranty duration, panel warranty, inverter warranty, battery warranty, installation warranty, and warranty claims.

Important Limitation:

Warranty terms vary by manufacturer, product, installer, country, and supplier. The chatbot should not promise warranty coverage without checking the actual warranty documentation.
